How It Works

From Browser to
Booked Measure

No quiz, no quote form, no phone tag — just a visitor designing their own wardrobe.

1

Upload their space

The visitor drops in a photo of the wall, alcove or closet they want fitted out.

2

Choose the essentials

They tap the features they want — rails, drawers, shelving, lighting — and add notes on style or size.

3

Instant render + AI consult

A photorealistic render of the wardrobe in their actual room appears, while an AI design consultant asks a couple of quick questions to lock in finish, doors and lighting.

4

Refine it live

They chat back and forth — "make it walnut," "add a mirror" — and re-render until it's right.

5

Book the measure

They leave their name, email, phone and suburb. The finished spec, render and photo land with your team as a booked, qualified lead.
